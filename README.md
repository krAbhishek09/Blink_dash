🛒 Blinkit Sales Dashboard (Power BI)
📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing the sales performance, customer satisfaction, and inventory distribution of Blinkit using Power BI.

The goal is to extract meaningful insights and identify opportunities for business optimization through data-driven decision-making.

🎯 Business Objective

To conduct a comprehensive analysis of:

Sales performance
Customer satisfaction
Inventory distribution

And identify key trends, patterns, and improvement areas using KPIs and visualizations.

📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
Total Sales
Overall revenue generated from all items sold.
Average Sales
Average revenue per transaction.
Number of Items
Total count of items sold.
Average Rating
Average customer rating for sold items.
📈 Dashboard Insights & Analysis
1. 🥑 Total Sales by Fat Content

Objective:
Analyze how fat content impacts total sales.

Insights Covered:

Comparison of sales based on fat categories (Low Fat, Regular)
Impact on:
Average Sales
Number of Items
Average Rating
2. 🛍️ Total Sales by Item Type

Objective:
Identify top-performing item categories.

Insights Covered:

Sales contribution by item types (Fruits, Snacks, Dairy, etc.)
KPI comparison across categories
3. 🏪 Fat Content by Outlet for Total Sales

Objective:
Compare outlet performance based on fat content segmentation.

Insights Covered:

Outlet-wise sales distribution
Fat category performance across locations
KPI variations across outlets
4. 🏢 Total Sales by Outlet Establishment

Objective:
Evaluate how outlet type or establishment year affects sales.

Insights Covered:

Sales trends based on outlet age/type
Identification of high-performing outlet segments
🧰 Tools & Technologies Used
Power BI – Data visualization & dashboard creation
Excel / Dataset – Data source
DAX – KPI calculations and measures
📌 Key Outcomes
Identified high-performing product categories
Analyzed customer preferences based on fat content
Evaluated outlet performance and growth opportunities
Provided actionable insights for business optimization
🚀 Conclusion

This dashboard helps in making data-driven decisions by providing a clear view of sales trends, customer behavior, and operational efficiency. It can be used to improve product strategy, inventory planning, and overall business performance.
